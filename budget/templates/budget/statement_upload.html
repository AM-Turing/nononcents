Statement PDF

Download the monthly statement PDF from your bank (not a scan or a photo) and choose it here. PDF statements from most banks work. The app reads the rows for each account and checks that they add up to the balances on the statement. If they do not add up, nothing is imported. If the statement has no balances to check against, the rows are staged marked "not verified" and you check each line. The PDF itself is not kept.
