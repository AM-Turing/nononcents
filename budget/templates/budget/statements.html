How the statement check works

Your entries stay the record. A bank statement is only a check that nothing was missed: each statement line is paired with an entry of the same account and amount dated up to 7 days before or 3 days after it. Tap Same purchase to confirm a pair; that marks the entry cleared. Merchant names on the statement are shown for reference only and never change your entries.
